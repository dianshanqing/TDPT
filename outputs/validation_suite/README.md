# Transport validation

`validation_summary.json` records 21 completed comparisons at absolute tolerance 2 × 10⁻¹¹. Seven physical inputs exercise a scaled duct, a uniform slab, a layered slab, vacuum, pure absorption, repeated scattering and a detector/boundary overlap. Baseline, compressed and full-history denote three reversible history encodings of each physical input. The suite also records three inverse/reflection checks and seven Monte Carlo diagnostics with 4,000 histories per physical case.

Use `../evidence_index.csv` to locate a detailed record by its case ID or encoding. The three scaled-duct files live in `../air_duct/gate_validation`. The 18 remaining files live in `gate_validation`. For each detailed file, match rows on the first six columns of `actual_joint` and `reference_joint` (status, x, y, z, direction and energy group). Missing states have probability zero. The seventh column is the probability. Summing over all coordinates except direction gives the direction marginal. The reference solver constructs the finite-step transition independently of the gate kernel.

`runtime.checkpoints` records step-end probability normalization, maximum joint error and the probability that any work qubit is nonzero. The largest final joint absolute error across the suite is 1.6653345369377348 × 10⁻¹⁶. All records report zero nonzero-work probability at the sampled step ends.

## Additional transport regressions

`small_circuit_logic` contains a separate test campaign. `transport_science.jsonl` records 45 transport checks and 54 traced step ends. `model_science.jsonl` has 18 model, encoding and horizon combinations. Both report zero maximum nonzero-work probability. Read the generating test files named in the index for the per-test input constructors. The three `quantum_ae_*.json` records check the inverse circuit and compare observed marked probabilities with the independent recursion for four predicates at powers 0, 1 and 2. Across 36 comparisons the maximum error is 1.1102230246251565 × 10⁻¹⁶. These checks exercise fixed Grover powers. The statistical stopping experiments are indexed under `amplitude_estimation`.

`classical_reference` contains the other 17 models from the classical screening suite and its summary CSV. The duct entry in the CSV resolves to `../air_duct/classical_reference/legacy_duct.json`. The `checkpoints` subdirectory contains separate longer-horizon runs for eight slab configurations. The resolved model physics in each archived classical record match the corresponding TDPT preset. Archived input-file hashes can refer to older expanded JSON documents. The present alias JSON and its expanded preset have separate identities.

`one_cell_mps` preserves two small MPS batches for the T2 one-cell case, covering bonds 16, 32 and 64 and truncation settings from 10⁻⁸ to 10⁻¹⁶. The raw files contain same-grid reference probabilities, readout values, backend settings and the strict-check outcome. Both batches finished and their strict absolute checks failed. For example, at bond 16 and truncation 10⁻¹² the detector relative error was 0.0741664. At bond 32 with the same truncation it was 6.63959 × 10⁻¹¹. The separate counts files contain sampled outcomes. Read `.json.gz` with gzip before decoding the JSON.
